About

I am a software engineer and technical leader whose route into software ran through submarine nuclear operations and industrial field engineering.

The arc

Personal account: I served aboard USS Philadelphia, studied nuclear engineering technology, supervised field work around heat exchangers, and began writing software to solve operating problems around me. That work became a software-development role, followed by engineering work in customer data, advertising, healthcare technology, and public-service APIs.

How I work

  • Make state visible. A system cannot be operated or migrated safely when its behavior is hidden.
  • Separate evidence from confidence. A useful narrative should say whether it comes from public source, repository source, attributed testimony, or personal account.
  • Design the handoff. Interfaces, runbooks, tests, and decision records are part of the system.
  • Prefer scoped outcomes. State what changed without inventing precision or exposing information that should remain private.
