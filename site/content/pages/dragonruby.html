DragonRuby Tutorial Experiment

A Rails-era tutorial series and browser packaging experiment preserved for historical inspection.

Source disposition

The 24 Markdown tutorial sources remain under dragonruby/. They are not currently rendered as public lesson routes by the static portfolio.

Artifact disposition

Historical browser loader, game data, and WebAssembly-related files remain under public/dragonruby/ as an archive. They are intentionally excluded from dist/.

Execution status

No claim is made that the archived browser build currently executes correctly, safely, or compatibly with this site's static serving headers. Restoring execution requires a separate provenance, licensing, header, and browser validation pass.
