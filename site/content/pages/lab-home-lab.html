Operational posture

Public ingress restrictions and alerting are in scope. Backup implementation is deferred and is not represented as complete. Dynamic telemetry must be allowlisted, delayed or coarsened where appropriate, rate-limited, and safe to replace with an unavailable state.

Solar as a measurement program

The planned program is a once-daily local collection job. Provider credentials and raw responses remain private; only reviewed daily or monthly aggregates may enter a static release. Electrical boundaries, units, coverage, and import/export semantics must be established before interpretation.

No environmental claim: This plan does not establish whole-home coverage, net energy, avoided emissions, carbon neutrality, or any other environmental outcome.
