Quartz

A compiled systems language with Ruby-like syntax, LLVM output, and a compiler written in Quartz.

What the pinned source establishes

At the revision used by this portfolio, Quartz compiles to LLVM IR without a garbage collector, virtual machine, or language runtime. Its self-hosted compiler compiles itself, and the repository documents byte-identical fixpoint verification.

Why it is part of this site

The portfolio's static-site generator is written in Quartz and compiled by a pinned clean Quartz checkout. That is a build-time relationship: ordinary page requests are static files served by Caddy, not programs executed by Quartz.

Inspect it

The separately assembled Quartz artifact is versioned independently from the core portfolio. Source is available from SourceHut. See Provenance for the exact pinned revision and validation scope.

Boundary

This page reports repository state at one pinned revision. It is not a portability, security, production-readiness, or language-stability attestation.
