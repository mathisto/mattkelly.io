Work case study

Legacy recovery before replacement

A migration starts by making the existing system understandable enough to change safely.

Situation

Personal account: At NexHealth from 2022 to 2023, I encountered a long-lived Python 2 and Django application, a decade-old diamond dependency, and a test suite that did not provide a trustworthy signal.

Approach

Personal account: The sequence was recovery first: untangle the dependency graph, repair tests, establish repeatable feedback, then port behavior toward Rails. That order reduced the chance of treating unknown behavior as disposable merely because it was old.

Scoped outcome

Personal account: The test suite returned to green and the application was moved toward Rails. This page does not claim a completion percentage, schedule, cost reduction, or production performance change.
