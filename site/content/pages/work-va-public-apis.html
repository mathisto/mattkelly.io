Work case study

VA and public APIs

Modernization work where reliability, privacy, and delivery controls are part of the product boundary.

Situation

Personal account: Beginning in 2023, I worked as a managing staff software engineer and technical lead with Ad Hoc on VA.gov. The archived resume describes an estate of Veterans Benefits APIs that needed security, performance, platform, and delivery modernization.

Work

Personal account: I audited and modernized API surfaces, led consolidation of legacy modules toward a greenfield Rails API-only platform, and designed delivery around GitHub Actions, Argo CD, and Kubernetes. The operative constraint was not novelty; it was making change legible and controlled in a federal environment handling PII and PHI.

Scoped outcome

Personal account: The work established a clearer platform direction and more automated container delivery. No numerical velocity, reliability, performance, or compliance outcome is claimed here because supporting records have not been approved for publication.

Public context

The Department of Veterans Affairs publishes the vets-api repository and developer documentation. Those links establish the public system context; they do not independently prove my contribution.

Disclosure boundary

No internal topology, control implementation, incident, vulnerability, private repository, beneficiary data, or customer-specific metric is disclosed.
